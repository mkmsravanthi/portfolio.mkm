My Internship at MedBeat

During my internship at MedBeat, I participated in the development and improvement of an existing healthcare application and preparations for expanding the application into the US market.

My internship consisted of three main phases: improving the existing Swedish application, researching the US healthcare market, and developing the US application with new backend services and integrations.

Phase 1 – Existing Swedish Application

In the first phase, I worked on improving the existing MedBeat healthcare application available in the Swedish market. My focus was on user interface improvements and making the application easier to use.

  • Added forward and backward arrows to carousel navigation, allowing users to move between items.
  • Implemented animations for conditional questions that appear when the user selects "Yes" in a previous question.
  • Worked on application updates and improvements for both iOS and Android.

Swedish App – UI Improvements

Phase 2 – US Healthcare Market Research

In the second phase, I participated in research and planning for launching MedBeat in the US healthcare market. I explored the healthcare environment, competitors, integrations and technical requirements needed for the new market.

  • Researched US healthcare applications and competitors.
  • Explored healthcare providers, healthcare data and patient referral processes.
  • Investigated healthcare integrations, Electronic Health Records (EHR) and HL7 FHIR.
  • Researched healthcare regulations and requirements, including HIPAA and FDA considerations.
  • Explored authentication solutions and cloud services for the US application.

US Market Research & Planning

Phase 3 – US Application Development

In the third phase, I worked on developing the US version of the application. My main responsibility was backend integration and data management for the iOS application.

  • Created a new AWS Amplify environment for the US application.
  • Created GraphQL data models for the backend.
  • Integrated the iOS application with AWS backend services using Amplify DataStore.
  • Implemented data saving and synchronisation between the iOS application and AWS backend.
  • Updated height and weight input fields to support decimal values.
  • Improved the presentation of measurement units in the user information screen.

Authentication and time-zone handling were also part of the US application development and were handled by other team members.

US Application – Backend & Integration
